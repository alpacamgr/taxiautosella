# Project Overview & Legacy Website Audit

Comprehensive technical, UX, and content audit of **Taxi Auto Sella** (`https://www.taxiautosella.it/en/`), originally built by Digiem in Val Gardena, South Tyrol, Italy.

* **Central 24/7 Dispatch Hotline**: `+39 0471 790033` (Fax: `+39 0471 790034`)
* **Email Dispatch**: `info@taxiautosella.it`
* **Scale**: Val Gardena’s largest taxi and bus consortium (35+ years active, 18 native local drivers, 25 modern Mercedes 4MATIC vehicles).
* **Operational Scope**: Year-round dual-season operations across the Dolomites, Trentino-Alto Adige, Italy, Austria (Innsbruck/Salzburg), Germany (Munich), and Switzerland (Zürich).

## 2. Legacy Website Architecture & Content Map

The legacy website structure was analyzed and cataloged into 6 core operational modules with 100% information fidelity:

```text
https://www.taxiautosella.it/en/
├── 1. Welcome & Home (24h high-season service, 18 local drivers, consortium reliability)
├── 2. Prices & online booking (Airport & railway station rate requests)
├── 3. Our vehicles (25 vehicles: Sedans, 8-pax Minibuses, 56-seat Coaches, Wheelchair Van, Bike Trailer, Film Unit)
├── 4. Taxi, Minibus & Bus (Comprehensive 9-service catalog)
├── 5. Organised tours & trips (Venice, Verona & Lake Garda, Innsbruck & Swarovski, Cortina, Bolzano/Merano)
├── 6. Questions & answers (13 FAQ topics covering flight delays, meet & greet, payment, ski gear)
└── 7. Members & Sponsors (18 driver members, 8 official sponsors, 10 Val Gardena nightlife venues)
```
