# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

1. **International Alpine Tourists & Skiers**: Families and groups flying into regional airports (Munich, Innsbruck, Verona, Venice, Milan, Bergamo, Bolzano) booking planned long-distance transfers with oversized ski/snowboard equipment and luggage.
2. **Luxury Chalet & 5-Star Hotel Guests**: High-net-worth travelers and VIP guests requiring executive private Mercedes chauffeur service with meet & greet.
3. **Local Ski Resort Visitors & Après-Ski Diners**: Guests in Val Gardena (Ortisei / St. Ulrich, Santa Cristina, Selva / Wolkenstein) needing immediate on-demand taxi dispatch to ski lifts or restaurants via WhatsApp / direct phone hotline.
4. **Hotel Concierges & Tour Operators**: B2B partners arranging guest arrivals, panoramic Dolomites tours, and day excursions.

## Product Purpose

Taxi Auto Sella modernizes its web presence to deliver a frictionless, high-conversion airport transfer booking experience (inspired by modern digital leaders like Welcome Pickups) while providing 24/7 on-demand local dispatch for the Val Gardena and Dolomiti Superski region.

## Positioning

Val Gardena’s largest and most established taxi and bus consortium (35+ years in operation, 18 native mountain drivers, 25 modern Mercedes 4MATIC all-wheel-drive vehicles) offering guaranteed winter mountain pass reliability, free live flight delay tracking, door-to-door hotel delivery, and all-inclusive transparent pricing with zero hidden surcharges.

## Operating Context

- **Winter & Summer Mountain Conditions**: High Alpine passes (Passo Sella, Passo Gardena, Passo Pordoi) requiring permanent 4MATIC 4x4 drivetrains, certified snow chains, and seasoned local chauffeurs.
- **Flight Arrivals & Delays**: International travelers landing across 9 gateways requiring real-time flight monitoring and terminal meet & greet with personalized name boards.
- **Dual-Track Conversion Funnel**:
  - *Track A (Planned Long-Distance Transfers)*: Dynamic online quote calculator, vehicle tier selection, ski equipment options.
  - *Track B (Urgent Local Rides)*: 1-tap WhatsApp and phone hotline for fast resort dispatch.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Fleet Capacity**: 25 vehicles ranging from Mercedes E-Class Sedans (1–3 pax), Mercedes V-Class & Vito 4MATIC Minibuses (up to 8 pax + ski boxes), to 16–56 passenger luxury coaches and wheelchair-accessible vans.
- **Service Coverage**: Airport transfers (Innsbruck, Munich, Verona, Venice, Bergamo, Milan, Bolzano, Bologna, Treviso), train stations (Bolzano, Bressanone, Ponte Gardena), local ski lifts (Seceda, Ciampinoi, Dantercepies, Saslong), and organized day excursions (Sella Ronda, Venice Lagoon, Verona Arena, Innsbruck).
- **Pricing & Inclusions**: Fixed transparent quotes including highway tolls, Alpine permits, flight delay waiting time, and baggage handling.

## Brand Commitments

- **Consortium Identity**: 18 local driver-partners rooted in Santa Cristina / Val Gardena.
- **Direct Hotline**: `+39 0471 790033` and WhatsApp available 24/7 during high season.
- **Multilingual Service**: Full operational fluency in English, Italian, German, and Ladin.
- **Payment Flexibility**: Onboard credit card POS terminals (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, Google Pay) and cash.

## Evidence on Hand

- Scraped legacy website assets and service catalog from `https://www.taxiautosella.it/en/` (Digiem build).
- Verified route distances, driving durations, and pricing models for all 9 regional gateways to Val Gardena.
- 3 interactive switchable design concepts implemented in code: Alpine Luxury, Modern Tech, and Dolomites Adventure.

## Product Principles

1. **Dual-Track Immediacy**: Never force a distressed local skier through a 5-step form; never force a planned airport traveler to wait days for a manual email quote.
2. **Radical Transparency**: Upfront fixed pricing with all tolls, permits, luggage, and flight delay tracking included.
3. **Local Authority Over Sterile Tech**: Celebrate the 18 local drivers and 25-vehicle consortium scale rather than masquerading as an anonymous tech aggregator.
4. **Alpine Winter Resilience**: 100% 4MATIC all-wheel-drive readiness built into every vehicle tier.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Accessible vehicle options with hydraulic ramps and ISO 4-point wheelchair restraints.
- High-contrast, WCAG 2.1 AA compliant typography across all 3 visual concepts.
